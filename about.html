About me关于我

Afternoon light is good for introductions. I'm an engineer who likes the moment software meets real hardware.下午的光最适合自我介绍。我是一个喜欢软件和真实硬件相遇那一刻的工程师。

I'm an Honours Electrical Engineering student at the University of Waterloo (Co-op), working mostly on embedded systems, real-time Linux and industrial control software.

我是滑铁卢大学电子工程（Co-op）的学生，主要做嵌入式系统、实时 Linux 和工业控制软件。

Most recently I interned at Sioux Technologies, calibrating firmware for production smart controllers and building an atomic rollback mechanism that took OS-level configuration errors down to zero. I like problems where software has to survive contact with real hardware: noisy CAN buses, microsecond scheduling, installs that can't be allowed to half-fail.

最近在 Sioux Technologies 实习，为生产环境的智能控制器标定固件，还写了一个原子回滚机制，把系统级配置错误降到了零。我喜欢软件必须和真实硬件打交道的问题：嘈杂的 CAN 总线、微秒级调度、绝不能装到一半失败的安装程序。

Outside work I'm on the Midnight Sun solar car team and help rebuild the website for Electrium Mobility, Waterloo's electric-vehicle design team. On my own time I make side projects, from C++ CLIs to WeChat mini-programs and Claude-powered web apps.

课外我在 Midnight Sun 太阳能赛车队，也在帮滑铁卢电动交通设计团队 Electrium Mobility 重做官网。业余时间写各种小项目，从 C++ 命令行工具到微信小程序，再到用 Claude 驱动的网页应用。
